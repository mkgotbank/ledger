✦ What's New
Version 1.5
📦
Custom Units — Any Business
Products now support Count/Unit tracking (bottles, pieces, boxes — anything). Choose your unit type when adding a product. Existing products continue working exactly as before.
💲
Per-Product Price Reference
Each product has its own price suggestions. Tap any row in Price Reference to set prices specific to that product.
💬
AI Chat Follow-Up
After loading your AI advice, ask follow-up questions directly in the Tips tab. The advisor knows your full product data and stays on topic.
✎
Edit Log Notes
Tap the pencil icon on any log entry to add or update its note — works on sales, restocks, personal use and expenses.
📊
Per-Product Tips & Stats
Quick Stats, Smart Suggestions and AI advice are now separate for each product. Each one remembers its own analysis.
