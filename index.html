App Icon
To show it on your home screen
Your pick updates inside the app right away. Phones lock the home-screen icon when you add the app, so to change it there:
1. Tap Back up now below (safe on every device).
2. Remove The Ledger from your home screen, then re-add it.
3. Reopen and tap Settings → Data & Sync → Load Sync Code if anything looks empty.
Install the App
Add The Ledger to your home screen — it opens full-screen and works completely offline, just like an app from the store. No download needed.
📱 iPhone & iPad — Safari
1Open this page in Safari, then tap the Share button (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
2Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”.
3Tap “Add” in the top corner — the Ledger icon lands on your home screen.
🤖 Android — Chrome
1Open this page in Chrome, then tap the menu (three dots) in the top-right corner.
2Tap “Install app”, or “Add to Home screen” if you don’t see it.
3Tap “Install” — the Ledger is added like any other app.
App Store & Google Play versions are coming soon.
Privacy Policy
Effective June 8, 2026 · The Ledger

The Ledger ("we", "us", the "App") is built privacy-first. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

1. Your data: on your device, and in your account

Until you create an account, the products, sales, expenses, invoices, business profile and settings you enter are stored only on your device (in your browser's storage). When you create an account and sign in, that data also syncs to your private space in our cloud database (Supabase) so you can reach it across devices. Each business's data is isolated from every other business's by database-level security. A copy stays on your device as an offline cache. If you clear your browser data or remove the App, the on-device copy is deleted — so keep your own backups.

2. Third-party services the App contacts

To work, the App connects to a few outside services:

  • Hosting & updates (GitHub): the App is delivered from, and checks for updates against, GitHub. Like any website visit, this involves standard technical data such as your IP address and device/browser type, handled under GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Accounts & cloud sync (Supabase): if you create an account, your email address and the business data you enter are stored in our cloud database and authentication provider (Supabase) so you can sign in and sync across devices. Your data is isolated from other businesses by database-level security and handled under Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google", Google verifies your identity and shares basic profile information (such as your name and email) to create or sign you into your account, under Google's privacy policy. You can always use an email and password instead.
  • AI Advisor (optional): if you use the AI Advisor, the question you ask and the figures needed to answer it are sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply, handled under that provider's policy. Do not enter anything you are not comfortable sending. If you add your own AI key, your request goes to that provider instead.
3. What we do NOT do

No accounts are required to use the App today. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or rent your information.

4. Backups and sync codes

Exports and "sync codes" are generated by you and stay in your control. If you share a backup or sync code with someone, they can read its contents — treat it like a copy of your books.

5. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you can access, edit, export or permanently delete it at any time from within the App. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU's GDPR and California's CPRA give you additional rights over personal information; to exercise any right regarding data we may process, contact us below.

6. Children

The App is intended for business owners and is not directed at children under 16.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, when optional cloud accounts and sync are introduced). We will revise the effective date above and note material changes in the App.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at qualityselectiion@gmail.com.
