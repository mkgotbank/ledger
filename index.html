Privacy Policy
Effective June 11, 2026 · The Ledger

The Ledger ("we", "us", the "App") is built privacy-first. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

In plain terms: your books are yours. They're stored on your device and — if you create an account — backed up to your own private cloud space so you can sync across devices. If you invite teammates, they see your business data based on the role you give them. We don't show ads, we don't track you, and we never sell your data.

1. Your data: on your device, and in your account

Until you create an account, the products, sales, expenses, invoices, business profile and settings you enter are stored only on your device (in your browser's local storage and on-device database). When you create an account and sign in, that data also syncs to your private space in our cloud database (Supabase) so you can reach it across devices. Each business's data is isolated from every other business's by database-level security. The App also keeps automatic on-device backups, and (when you're signed in) recent cloud backups, so your data can be restored if something goes wrong. When you delete an entry, invoice or product (or rename or reset a product), its contents are removed; a small deletion marker (an internal reference number or the former product name only — no other business details) is kept for up to 120 days so your other devices remove their copy too instead of restoring it. If you are signed in, your data remains in your cloud account even if you remove the App from a device. Signing out keeps a copy of your books on that device so signing back in is instant; it is shown again only after the same account signs in, and it is removed automatically if a different account signs in on that device. To clear a device completely (for example before lending or selling it), use Wipe all data under More. If you use the App without an account and then clear your browser data or remove the App, the on-device copy is deleted — so create an account or export a backup to keep your data safe.

2. Team members and shared access

You can invite people to your business by email and give each one a role — Owner, Admin, Staff or Viewer. Anyone you invite can access your business's data to the extent their role allows (for example, a Viewer can only read; a Staff member can record sales and expenses). We store each member's email address and role so the App can enforce those permissions. You are responsible for who you invite and the role you grant them; you can change a member's role or remove them at any time from Team & roles. Removing a member ends their access going forward. When a team member records a sale, expense, restock or other entry, our servers record which member created it — stamped from their verified sign-in, so the entry's author cannot be altered or faked — and this is shown to owners and admins in the Audit log so there is a reliable record of who did what.

If you belong to more than one business, you can switch between them from More → Switch business. Each business's data is kept completely separate: switching only changes which business you are viewing, and your access to each is always governed by your role in that business.

3. Third-party services the App contacts

To work, the App connects to a few outside services:

  • Hosting & updates (GitHub): the App is delivered from, and checks for updates against, GitHub. Like any website visit, this involves standard technical data such as your IP address and device/browser type, handled under GitHub's privacy policy. Our informational website (linked from More → Visit our website) is hosted the same way; it has no accounts, no forms and no analytics of ours, and nothing you do in the App is sent to it.
  • Accounts & cloud sync (Supabase): if you create an account, your email address and the business data you enter are stored in our cloud database and authentication provider (Supabase) so you can sign in and sync across devices. Your data is isolated from other businesses by database-level security and handled under Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google", Google verifies your identity and shares basic profile information (such as your name and email) to create or sign you into your account, under Google's privacy policy. You can always use an email and password instead.
  • AI Advisor (optional): if you use the AI Advisor or its chat — including the ready-made questions and questions about how to use the App — your question, the recent messages in that conversation, and the figures needed to answer are sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply, handled under that provider's policy. Do not enter anything you are not comfortable sending. If you add your own AI key, your requests go to that provider instead; the key itself is stored only on your device.
4. What we do NOT do

We do not show ads, we do not use analytics or tracking cookies, and we never sell or rent your information. The App also asks your browser to treat its storage as persistent, purely so your on-device data is not evicted by the operating system — this does not give us any access to anything else on your device.

5. Backups, exports and sync codes

The App automatically keeps recent backups on your device, and you can export a full backup file or copy a "sync code" at any time from Data & Sync. Exports and sync codes are generated by you and stay in your control — if you share one, the recipient can read its contents, so treat it like a copy of your books.

6. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you can access, edit, export or permanently delete it at any time from within the App. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as Canada's PIPEDA, the EU's GDPR and California's CPRA give you additional rights over personal information; to exercise any right regarding data we may process, contact us below.

7. Children

The App is intended for business owners and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, as cloud accounts, sync and backups develop). We will revise the effective date above and note material changes in the App.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at qualityselectiion@gmail.com.
